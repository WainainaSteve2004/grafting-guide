Grafting Methods

Choose the right technical method for your stem diameter and season.

Cleft Grafting

Ideal for top-working older rootstocks or branches with larger stem diameters.

T-Budding

Popular for citrus and stone fruits during active spring/summer sap flow.

Whip & Tongue

Provides strong cambium contact for young branches of identical thickness.

Side Veneer

Excellent for evergreen fruit trees like mangoes and avocados in warm weather.
