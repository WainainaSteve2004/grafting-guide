🥭 Mango Grafting

About Mango Grafting

Mango grafting is a useful technique for producing healthy and productive mango trees. It allows growers to combine a strong rootstock with a desirable mango variety.

Why Graft Mango Trees?

  • Produces trees with desirable fruit qualities.
  • Can help trees produce fruit earlier.
  • Helps maintain good varieties.
  • Produces productive and healthy trees.

Basic Grafting Steps

  1. Select a healthy rootstock.
  2. Choose a healthy scion from the desired mango variety.
  3. Make clean and matching cuts.
  4. Join the scion and rootstock carefully.
  5. Secure the graft with grafting tape.
  6. Keep the graft protected while it heals.

Mango Tree Care

Provide adequate water, good soil drainage and sufficient sunlight. Remove unwanted shoots below the graft and monitor the graft regularly for signs of growth or disease.
