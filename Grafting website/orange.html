🍊 Orange Grafting

About Orange Grafting

Orange grafting is a useful method of producing healthy and productive orange trees. It combines a strong rootstock with a desirable orange variety.

Why Graft Orange Trees?

  • Produces trees with desirable fruit qualities.
  • Can help trees produce fruit earlier.
  • Helps maintain good orange varieties.
  • Can produce strong and productive trees.

Basic Grafting Steps

  1. Select a healthy orange rootstock.
  2. Choose a healthy scion from the desired variety.
  3. Make clean and matching cuts.
  4. Join the scion and rootstock carefully.
  5. Secure the graft with grafting tape.
  6. Protect the graft while it heals.

Orange Tree Care

Provide adequate water, good drainage and sufficient sunlight. Remove unwanted shoots below the graft and regularly check the tree for pests, diseases and healthy new growth.
