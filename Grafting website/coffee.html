Coffee Grafting

About Coffee Grafting

Coffee grafting is a propagation technique that can be used to combine desirable characteristics of different coffee plants. It can help growers establish vigorous plants while maintaining desirable coffee varieties.

Why Graft Coffee Plants?

  • Can combine desirable plant characteristics.
  • Can help establish vigorous plants.
  • Can help maintain selected coffee varieties.
  • Can support productive coffee production.

Basic Grafting Steps

  1. Select a healthy coffee rootstock.
  2. Choose healthy scion material.
  3. Make clean and matching cuts.
  4. Join the scion and rootstock carefully.
  5. Secure the graft properly.
  6. Protect the graft while it heals.

Coffee Plant Care

Provide suitable moisture, good drainage and appropriate growing conditions. Keep the graft protected and monitor the plant regularly for healthy new growth.
