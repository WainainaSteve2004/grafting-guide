Grafting Techniques & Methods

Master step-by-step techniques used by horticulturists to graft different woody plants and fruit trees successfully.

Cleft Grafting

Ideal for top-working mature trees or joining scions to larger rootstocks.

Whip & Tongue

A strong structural join used when scion and rootstock are equal in diameter.

T-Budding

A fast, popular technique ideal for citrus trees during active growth seasons.

Side Veneer Grafting

Popular for mangoes and evergreen species where the rootstock stem is preserved.
