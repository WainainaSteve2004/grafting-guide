Bud Grafting (T-Budding)

Bud grafting uses a single vegetative bud instead of a stem scion stick. It is highly efficient and commonly used in mid-to-late summer when the bark slips easily.

Tools Needed

  • Budding knife
  • Budding tape or rubber ties

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. T-Cut on Rootstock: Make a vertical cut through the bark, followed by a horizontal cross cut at the top to form a "T".
  2. Slice the Bud: Slice a healthy bud shield off the desired cultivar stick, including a small shield of bark.
  3. Insert Bud: Gently peel open the flaps of the T-cut on the rootstock and slide the bud down into the slot.
  4. Wrap Securely: Wrap tightly above and below the bud eye with budding tape, leaving the bud itself exposed.
