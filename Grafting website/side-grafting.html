Side Grafting Technique

Side grafting allows insertion of a scion into the side of a rootstock trunk or branch without severing the top of the rootstock until after the union has healed.

Tools Needed

  • Sharp grafting knife
  • Grafting tape
  • Tree seal compound

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare Stock Cut: Make a downward, slanting cut into the side of the rootstock stem, about 1 to 1.5 inches deep.
  2. Prepare Scion: Cut the base of the scion into a sharp, thin wedge with one side slightly longer.
  3. Insert Scion: Bend the stock stem slightly back to open the side cut, and insert the scion wedge, aligning the cambium.
  4. Bind & Prune: Bind tightly with tape. Once growth begins, cut back the rootstock top above the graft.
