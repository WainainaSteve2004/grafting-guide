Apple Grafting

About Apple Grafting

Apple grafting is a useful technique for producing healthy and productive apple trees. It allows growers to combine a strong rootstock with a desirable apple variety.

Why Graft Apple Trees?

  • Produces healthy and productive apple trees.
  • Can help trees produce fruit earlier.
  • Helps maintain desirable apple varieties.
  • Can improve tree strength and fruit quality.

Basic Grafting Steps

  1. Select a healthy apple rootstock.
  2. Choose a healthy scion from the desired apple variety.
  3. Make clean and matching cuts.
  4. Join the scion and rootstock carefully.
  5. Secure the graft with grafting tape.
  6. Protect the graft while it heals.

Apple Tree Care

Provide adequate water, good soil drainage and sufficient sunlight. Remove unwanted shoots below the graft and check the graft regularly for healthy growth.
