Avocado Grafting

About Avocado Grafting

Avocado grafting is a useful technique for producing healthy and productive avocado trees. It combines a strong rootstock with a desirable avocado variety.

Why Graft Avocado Trees?

  • Produces healthy and productive avocado trees.
  • Can help trees produce fruit earlier.
  • Helps maintain desirable avocado varieties.
  • Can improve tree performance and fruit quality.

Basic Grafting Steps

  1. Select a healthy avocado rootstock.
  2. Choose a healthy scion from the desired avocado variety.
  3. Make clean and matching cuts.
  4. Join the scion and rootstock carefully.
  5. Secure the graft with grafting tape.
  6. Protect the graft while it heals.

Avocado Tree Care

Provide adequate water, good soil drainage and sufficient sunlight. Remove unwanted shoots below the graft and check the graft regularly for healthy growth.
