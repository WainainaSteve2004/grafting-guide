Whip-and-Tongue Grafting

This technique provides maximum cambium layer contact and high structural strength, making it ideal for young rootstocks and scions of matching thickness.

Tools Needed

  • Razor-sharp grafting knife
  • Grafting tape or rubber strips
  • Sealant or wax

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Slanting Cut: Make a smooth, diagonal cut about 1 to 1.5 inches long on both rootstock and scion.
  2. Cut the Tongues: On both cut surfaces, make a downward slice starting one-third from the tip to form interlocking "tongues."
  3. Interlock: Slide the scion and rootstock together so the tongues interlock firmly with cambium layers aligned.
  4. Wrap & Seal: Secure tightly with grafting tape and apply sealant.
