🍋 Lemon Grafting

About Lemon Grafting

Lemon grafting is a useful technique for producing healthy, productive and strong lemon trees. It combines a suitable rootstock with a desirable lemon variety.

Why Graft Lemon Trees?

  • Produces healthy and productive lemon trees.
  • Can help trees produce fruit earlier.
  • Allows growers to maintain desirable lemon varieties.
  • Can improve tree strength and performance.

Basic Grafting Steps

  1. Select a healthy lemon rootstock.
  2. Choose a healthy scion from the desired lemon variety.
  3. Make clean and matching cuts.
  4. Join the scion and rootstock carefully.
  5. Secure the graft with grafting tape.
  6. Protect the graft while it heals.

Lemon Tree Care

Provide adequate water, good soil drainage and sufficient sunlight. Remove unwanted shoots below the graft and regularly check the graft for healthy growth.
