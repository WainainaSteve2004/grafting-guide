Cleft Grafting Technique

Cleft grafting is one of the oldest and most popular methods used for top-working mature fruit trees or joining small scions to established rootstocks, typically in early spring before growth begins.

Tools Needed

  • Sharp grafting knife & grafting cleaver/chisel
  • Mallet or small hammer
  • Grafting wax or tape

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare Rootstock: Cut the branch or trunk cleanly across at a right angle.
  2. Split the Stock: Use a grafting chisel and mallet to make a vertical split (cleft) 2–3 inches deep.
  3. Shape the Scions: Cut two scion sticks with 2–3 buds each into long, smooth wedges.
  4. Insert Scions: Wedge open the split and insert the scions on either side, carefully matching the cambium layers.
  5. Seal: Coat all exposed cut surfaces with grafting wax to prevent moisture loss.
